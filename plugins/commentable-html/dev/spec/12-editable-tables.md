## Editable tables

Opt-in `data-cmh-table` tables are document-scale plain-text/choice editors, not spreadsheets.
They use stable table, column and row identities. Their cells are excluded from offset commenting
and stable-prose validation hashes, like editable notes; ordinary tables remain unchanged.
