# Example prompt - Before and after flow comparison

## Prompt

> Make a commentable HTML report comparing raw-data caching with prepared-result caching.
> Use two tall Mermaid flowcharts side by side at readable size, with equal-width columns
> and compact spacing. Keep the comparison side by side on phones with horizontal scrolling,
> not tiny fixed-height gallery thumbnails. Do not add invisible nodes to align steps.

## What you get

The companion `report-comparison.html` demonstrates the shipped comparison layout.
Hover diagram nodes to comment, or focus a diagram and press Enter.
